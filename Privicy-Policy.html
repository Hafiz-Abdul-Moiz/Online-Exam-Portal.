1. Global Input Restrictions & Peripheral Controls

To prevent unauthorized external assistance, the following system-level restrictions are enforced across the entire portal:

Globally Blocked Mouse Right-Click (Context Menu)

Poori website par mouse ka right click completely disabled hai. Inspect Element, save image, ya inspect menu access nahi kiya ja sakta.

Globally Blocked Keyboard System Shortcuts

Alt+Tab, Esc, Windows/Command Key, F1 se F12 keys, Ctrl+C (Copy), Ctrl+V (Paste), Ctrl+U (Source code), aur Ctrl+Shift+I (DevTools) completely locked hain.

Instant Termination Tab-Switching & Window Minimization

Exam ke dauran doosra browser tab open karna, application minimize karna, ya window focus hatana foran AI Sentinel breach count karta hai.

Strictly Prohibited Screenshots & Screen Recording

PrintScreen key, third-party screen capturing, screen recording utilities, ya mirroring devices se paper copy karna cyber offense shumar hota hai.

5. Dynamic Question Shuffling & Multi-Version Anti-Peeping Protocol

To eliminate physical glance-copying and adjacent screen peeping in exam halls and computer laboratories, SkillTester deploys an automated Dynamic Seeded Randomization Engine:

Har candidate ka randomized paper student ID aur secure seed ke zariye bind hota hai. Answer evaluation usi specific seed ke relative order par automatically hoti hai, jisse 100% fair aur transparent marking ensure hoti hai.

In bona-fide emergency situations (medical distress, sudden illness, or urgent restroom break during long sessions), the Autonomous Board provides a secure Emergency Pause & Time-Extension Mechanism governed by strict proctor oversight:
